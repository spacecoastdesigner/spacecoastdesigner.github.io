🔒 Privacy Policy

Space Coast Designer Apps

The short version: our apps work offline. We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information. What happens on your device stays on your device.

Effective October 29, 2026

🧭 Who we are

Space Coast Designer Apps ("we", "us", "our") makes mobile apps and websites. This policy covers every app and website we publish, unless that app shows its own policy instead.

🏠 No account, no data collection

  • No sign-up, no login, no profile — our apps never ask who you are.
  • We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, contacts, photos, precise location, or browsing history.
  • No analytics or tracking tools run inside our apps.
  • Content is bundled inside the app, so it can be read with no internet connection.

📱 What the app uses on your device

  • Motion sensors: some apps tilt or move with your phone. Motion data is read on your device only — never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Keep screen awake: the screen stays on while the app is open.
  • On-device storage: the app remembers what you unlocked or last looked at, on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

🛒 Purchases

Upgrades and content packs are paid through Google Play. We never see your card number, billing address, or Google account — Google's privacy policy covers that payment data. Your unlock status is kept on your device.

📢 Ads in the free version

The free version of an app may show ads from Google AdMob (banner ads and occasional full-screen ads). AdMob may use device identifiers to serve and measure ads.

Because our apps are made for kids and families, ads are requested under a "made for kids" configuration: no personalized ads, and Google limits the data it processes accordingly.

Buying an upgrade removes ads completely.

Google's policies: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads

🧒 Kids and families

Our apps are designed for all ages, including children. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and our ad serving is configured to meet the requirements of COPPA and GDPR-K for kids' and family apps.

🚫 We don't sell data

We don't sell, rent, or share personal information — because we don't collect any. There is nothing for us to hand to advertisers.

🎛️ Your choices

  • Remove ads: buy an upgrade in the app.
  • Ad settings: reset or limit the advertising ID in your device's Google Ads settings.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

📬 Questions?

Email us at space.coast.designer.apps@gmail.com and a real person will answer.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
